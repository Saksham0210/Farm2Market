How it flows

  1. Farmer/FPO registers and lists produce — quantity, quality, available date, pickup location.
  2. Buyers (bulk or individual) browse the marketplace and place an order.
  3. The Smart Order Engine matches inventory, checks location & delivery time, and batches nearby individual orders.
  4. Smart Logistics Selection picks the best/cheapest partner and optimizes the pickup + delivery route.
  5. Delivery is confirmed with an OTP, which triggers automatic payment settlement for the farmer and logistics partner.
