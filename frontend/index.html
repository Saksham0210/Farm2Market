How Farm Circle works

  1. Farmers and FPOs create an account and list their products.
  2. Consumers browse available products directly from farmers.
  3. Buyers add products to their cart and place an order.
  4. The platform manages pickup and delivery through logistics partners.
  5. Farmers receive orders directly without unnecessary middlemen.
